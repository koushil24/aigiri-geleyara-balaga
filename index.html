About AIGIRI GELEYARA BALAGA

Serving Society Through Friendship, Culture & Unity

AIGIRI GELEYARA BALAGA (AGB), Mysuru is a youth community organization established in 2025 with the vision of bringing people together through friendship, culture, spirituality, and social service.

Since its inception, AGB has been actively organizing religious celebrations, cultural events, social activities, and community service initiatives that strengthen unity and promote positive values among youth and society.

Our flagship celebrations include Ganeshotsava, Ashada Shukravara Pooja Mahotsava, and various future initiatives dedicated to serving society, preserving traditions, and encouraging volunteerism.

We believe that true friendship becomes meaningful when it inspires service, respect, leadership, and collective responsibility. Every member of AGB contributes towards building a stronger, united, and compassionate community.
